# Agent_13 report

## 1. Agent ID and persona
Agent_13, simulated persona "Mr. Aslam", 58, manager at a family-run manufacturing firm. App copy `ua-13`, port 7953. (Simulated persona; reactions are plausible, not facts about real people.)

## 2. Background and relevant characteristics
Windows desktop, Word, Excel, email. Files are scattered; he saves "wherever it opens". Distrusts new software ("it will mess up my files") and calls AI "a fad". Normally asks his nephew. Fixed habits, little time. Would adopt only something nearly invisible and clearly harmless. Plain speaker, confused by jargon.

## 3. Initial expectations (before using it)
"A helper that lives in my computer and only sees folders I pick. Fine. But I do not want it moving my files. My files are where I know them to be, even if it looks messy to you. 'Tidy a messy folder' is exactly what I am afraid of. 'AI model runs on your computer' means nothing to me. If it asks before it does anything, maybe. Why would I not just ask my nephew or use Windows search?" Doubts: will it break file names my staff rely on; is it one more thing to learn; does it send my price lists somewhere.

## 4. Interaction and exploration summary
Started the app, unticked "Voice is on" (a voice greeting and microphone box looked odd). Went to Folders and typed the path of an invented messy folder (15 files: invoices, budget xlsx, resume copies, screenshots, holiday photos, zip). Asked two plain questions. Pressed "Suggest a tidy-up" and read the list of steps (first session ended before I approved). In a second session (app restarted, folder re-added) I pressed Suggest a tidy-up again, approved it, checked the folder, pressed "What changed", then "Undo last change", and checked the folder again. Looked at "What it knows" and Settings. Did not try: drafting, email, web search, remote access, cloud keys, saved notes, repeated tasks, "Approve, don't ask again". Voice was not tested (no microphone, cannot hear audio).

## 6. Feedback about the research idea (as the persona)
"Showing me the list before it does anything, and putting everything back with one button, that I understand. That is the only reason I did not close it. I like that it only sees the folder I give it. But honestly my problem is not that computers can see too much. My problem is I cannot find a file when I need it. And tidying up, the very thing it offers first, is what I do not want: the staff know where things are even if it looks like a mess to you. If it just answered 'where is that file' reliably, I would use that. Everything else I would leave."

## 7. Perceived problem relevance
3 of 5. Finding files and "where did I save it" is real and happens most weeks, mildly annoying, not a crisis. Interruptions are part of the job. A messy folder does not bother me; it bothers the nephew. Tidying is a low need for me.

## 8. Perceived value of the proposed solution
2.5 of 5. Valuable: ask before change, visible list, Undo, "nothing is deleted", I can erase what it knows. Not valuable to me: tidy-up (I do not want files moved), voice, cloud and email set-up, anything with keys and passwords. Finding files by asking was the useful piece, but my test answer on "latest invoice" failed.

## 9. Adoption likelihood and reasoning
**Might try**, only if my nephew installed and set it up and it did nothing on its own. I would not keep using it unless the "where is my file" answer is right nearly every time and plainly worded. What would change my mind: a simple one-screen version with just Ask; proof it never touches the folders unless I press a button; my nephew's recommendation. What would make me avoid it: any surprise movement of files, jargon, being asked for passwords or keys.

## 10. Concerns, objections and suggestions
- Moving files is risky for me even with undo; I would want "never move, only find" as the default.
- Undo worked and the listing confirmed it, but one success does not remove my fear; I would want weeks of it not misbehaving.
- To be "invisible", voice and the greeting should be off by default.
- Plain words needed: "model", "cloud", "gemma", "Credential Manager", "remote access" mean nothing to me.
- "Approve, don't ask again" worries me; I would never press it.

## 11. Final assessment of the central claim
(a) Trust more than a full-access assistant: **partly**. Limiting to chosen folders and asking first calms me more than something that sees the whole computer, but I would not trust any AI by default; the Undo button did more for my trust than the folder limit.
(b) More willing to use it: **partly**. Yes compared with a full-access one (which I would refuse); but I am still not eager to use any of them.
(c) Limitation costs too much usefulness: **disagree** for me. My files are mostly in a few folders anyway; the limit does not cost me much.

Short answers:
- Are people really losing time to interruptions and routine file work: **partly**. Looking for files, yes; routine tidying, not for me.
- Is wanting broad access a real barrier for me: **agree**. I would not give anything the whole computer.
- Accept one mistake if undo is easy: **partly**. One mistake with a working undo, yes; two, I stop.
- Ask before every change or act and let me undo: **ask first**, definitely, for someone like me.
- Abilities I would use: finding files and answering "where is..."; maybe drafting a letter (not tried). Not tidy-up, voice, web, email.
- What makes setup too much effort: typing a folder path, any keys/passwords/app passwords, choosing models, anything I cannot do without my nephew.

## 12. Implementation limitations I ran into
- The folder list was empty again after the app was restarted; I had to add the folder again.
- "Latest invoice" returned "NOT FOUND" though invoice files exist (the invented files hold only tiny placeholder text).
- Budget answer wording was odd and gave no folder location.
- Work page is crowded; voice greeting and "No microphone found" messages appear on start-up.
- File counts changed (15, then 16/18/19) because my own screenshots were saved into the test folder and the app counted them; a test set-up effect, not necessarily a bug.
- Settings showed Gemini "Key saved" before I had set anything; I did not touch it.
